Under the hood

Built to count leads

Call tracking

One number, set once in assets/site.js → RT_CONFIG, stamped onto every [data-call] link and [data-num] label. Each tap pushes call_click with page + placement (header / hero / sticky / footer / quote) to dataLayer.

Schema

Every page: AutomotiveBusiness + EmergencyService (24/7 hours, areaServed, telephone = tracking number). Service & town pages add Service + BreadcrumbList. All pages with Q&A add FAQPage matching the visible FAQ. No AggregateRating — no fake reviews.

Backup CTA

Quote form (name, mobile, service, pickup, drop-off, vehicle, when) on every page, anchored #quote. Pushes quote_submit; wire to email/CRM at RT_CONFIG.leadEmail. “Show my GPS” button pushes gps_located.

Internal linking

Service grid on every page (all 14 services), town grid on every page, footer lists all services + towns, breadcrumb to home, plus the big “Back to main page” block.

Real-Fayetteville rule

Each page names real roads & landmarks: I-95 exits 40–56, Bragg Blvd, Skibo, Raeford Rd, Owen Dr, All American Fwy, Outer Loop, the Randolph / Reilly / All American gates, Cross Creek Mall, Cape Fear Valley.

Tenant slots

No invented years, prices, or testimonials. The About page has a dashed Operator details slot for the renter’s company, license, insurance and real truck photos.
